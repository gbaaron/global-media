About

Not a template shop. A studio.

Global Media is a hand-coded web studio for the entertainment side of the internet — musicians, podcasters, leagues, athletes, and personalities who want more than a Linktree.

What we actually do

We build custom sites from scratch — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Airtable, Netlify. No WordPress themes, no Wix, no template kits. Every client gets a site that looks and behaves like their brand specifically, because it was written specifically for them.

The pitch is simple: we spec it free, build a live preview you can log into, and you only pay when it's ready to go live on your domain. No one's locked in, no one's paying upfront for a site that doesn't exist yet.

Once you're live, we can keep going — content pipelines, feature adds, analytics, live-stats automation. Or we hand you the keys and walk away. Your call.
