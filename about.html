About

I'm Aaron, the person behind Global Media.

No faceless agency, no templates. Every project has me personally involved from the first idea to launch. I lead the direction, stay hands-on through the build, and make sure your site ends up feeling like you.

Why I started this

A lot of talented people (musicians, athletes, podcasters, small leagues) end up with a generic template or a link-in-bio page that doesn't reflect who they are. Global Media is my answer to that.

Every site is built by hand in real code: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Airtable, Netlify. No drag-and-drop builders, no theme kits. The result is a site made for you specifically: your audience, your voice, the way you actually work.

What I actually build

This isn't a landing page with a pretty header and nothing behind it. Here's the real work that goes into every project I take on:

Hand-coded, from a blank file

Every page is written from scratch, so nothing is bloated, broken, or borrowed from a template someone else is also using.

Real accounts, memberships & fan tiers

Logins, member perks, points, and paid tiers. Actual working systems, not screenshots of a feature you'll "get later."

Live data & analytics behind the scenes

Content pipelines, engagement tracking, and dashboards so you can see what your audience actually does, not vanity numbers.

A smart helper bot on every site

A built-in assistant that answers your fans' or customers' questions in your voice, day or night.

Free preview before you pay a cent

I spec it, build a live version you can actually log into and click around, and you only pay when it's ready to go live on your domain.
